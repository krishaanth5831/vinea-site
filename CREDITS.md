Geist and Geist Mono come in through `next/font`, which downloads them at build
time and serves them from this domain. Geist Pixel is self-hosted from
`src/fonts/`. No font CDN is contacted at runtime.

## Everything else

The Vinea mark is the diamond pixel logo chosen in June 2026, redrawn as SVG
for this site. The thread, the pixel arrow and the refusal drawings are drawn
