| Face | Use | Licence |

General Sans is self-hosted from `src/fonts/`. IBM Plex Mono is pulled in
through `next/font`, which downloads it at build time and serves it from this
domain too — no font CDN is contacted at runtime.

## Everything else

The schematic of the machine, the tool heads and the crop field behind the
